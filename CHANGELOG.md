- **Internal audits (backend; slice 5).** An **audit** (`AU-0007`, the id like `NC-`) is a planned check of one part of the chart: a title, the **scope node** (it and everything beneath it), a named **lead auditor**, a planned date, and — once done — a written summary. Status `PLANNED → IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED`, or `CANCELLED` from either of the first two (reason required); nothing is deleted. **Who:** planning, editing and cancelling belong to `manage_quality` alone — an independent function, so the مسئول of a unit does not schedule or call off the audit of their own unit; starting, completing and raising findings belong to the lead auditor or `manage_quality`; reading is scoped like records (`visible_audits`: `manage_quality`, the lead auditor, or a مسئول of the scope node or any node above it — a مسئول of a بخش *beneath* the scope sees nothing — and anyone else gets a 404, never a 403). **Findings are non-conformances:** `POST /quality/audits/{id}/findings/` files one with source AUDIT and `audit` set, owned by the audited node or any node beneath it (anything outside is refused), only while the audit is running; observations are simply MINOR. A finding has **one** history line, `finding_raised`, on both the audit's timeline and the record's — not that line plus a separate `nc_reported`. The lead auditor can also read the findings of their audit (so they keep sight of what a quality manager raised for them), and `GET /quality/nonconformances/?audit=<id>` lists them. - **Corrective actions, closing and reopening (backend; slice 2).** A **corrective action** (اقدام اصلاحی) is one fix for a non-conformance: title, description, **one** assignee (unlike a project objective's several — one accountable name per fix is the ISO norm), a required deadline, a status, and the verification trail. Status: `TODO ↔ IN_PROGRESS → DONE`, moved by the assignee; then a **different manager verifies it** (`VERIFIED`) or sends it back (`IN_PROGRESS`, reason required); `CANCELLED` is the way out of an action no longer needed. Nothing is ever deleted. **The rules, all enforced in `services.py`, not just the views:** actions are added, reassigned, re-dated, edited and cancelled only by the record's manager and only while the record is «در دست اقدام» (`409 nc_not_in_progress` otherwise); the assignee may change **the status and only the status** (the objective-assignee rule — a request mixing status with anything else needs the manager); the status graph has no shortcuts (`TODO → DONE` is `409 invalid_transition`; VERIFIED and CANCELLED are reachable only through their own endpoints); **whoever did the work cannot verify it — not even the مدیر عامل** (`409 self_verification`; if the only manager is the assignee, another manager must); an assignee must be active and not the developer account; a deadline may be today but not the past; a finished (verified or cancelled) action no longer changes. **Closing** (`POST …/close/`) follows the rule the owner chose: **at least one action, every non-cancelled action verified, and a written effectiveness note** — refused with `409 cannot_close` carrying the counts (`actions`, `verified`, `unverified`) so the screen can say exactly what is missing; a cancelled action neither blocks nor counts, and a record with only cancelled actions has no plan. The check runs **under the record's row lock**: without it, closing could count «۱ از ۱ تایید شده» while a second request adds an unverified action in that instant and the record closes with work open (a real-threads test fails 3 runs of 3 with the lock removed). **Reopening** now also takes a CLOSED record back to IN_PROGRESS (a rejected one still goes to OPEN): its verified actions stay verified, and the effectiveness note is cleared from the record — the closing line in the history keeps it — so it never reads as true of a record that is open again. **Derived, never stored:** `actions_total` (non-cancelled), `actions_verified`, `actions_overdue` (still the assignee's to do *and* past the deadline — a DONE action waits on a verifier, it is not late) come from aggregate subqueries, so a list of any length is **three queries** (tested); `?overdue=1` and `?mine=assigned|manage` filter on them. **Visibility** gains the assignee (an `Exists`, not a join, so a record is not repeated once per action): whoever currently holds an action can read its record and no more — reassign it away and they are back to a 404. History gains the action kinds and `action_edited` (a small addition to the plan: edits to what an action says matter as much as edits to a report); `action_due_changed` carries ISO dates in the status fields and so no label. Notifications (Phase 15, eight new kinds): the assignee is told when an action is assigned, verified, fails verification or is cancelled; marking one DONE tells the node chain's leads **except the person who did it**; closing tells the reporter and every assignee, never the closer. A new idempotent beat task, `notifications.check_due_actions` (07:35), reminds an assignee once a day while an action is due soon or overdue — only actions still theirs to do on a record still being worked. Personnel with an assigned action cannot be deleted (`user_has_quality_records`, as for reporters). Tests: 53 new (92 in `apps/quality`; the shared fixtures moved to `quality/test_support.py`): the whole assignee/manager/verifier matrix, derived counts including the three-query bound, close, reopen, the beat task, and a real-threads race. **Mutation-checked**: closing despite unverified actions, closing with none, a cancelled action blocking closing, the assignee verifying their own work, an assignee editing more than the status, and reminders ignoring the record's status each fail tests; removing the row lock fails the race test. Migrations `quality/0002` and `notifications/0004` read and applied; exercised end to end against the dev database inside a transaction that was rolled back (report → accept → add action → close refused → beat task → counts), leaving no data. **Not built yet:** every screen (slices 3–4), audits and risks (5–8), the quality figures in Reports (9).

### Phase 17 — reporting (owner's roadmap, 2026-10-01)
- **«شاخص‌های کلیدی»: the company KPI numbers — how long documents wait, how often reviewers send them back, where projects stand, who is loaded.** `GET /reports/kpi/` (gated by `view_reports`; under `/reports/` rather than the roadmap's `/dashboard/kpi/` so `apps.dashboard` does not become a hub for every app) computes everything at read time from rows that already exist — **no new model, nothing stored**, because a stored KPI goes stale the moment someone edits history. The definitions are in `apps/reports/kpi.py` and repeated on the screen beside each number, so no one has to guess: the **confirm step** is SUBMITTED → the next CONFIRMED of the same document, the **approve step** CONFIRMED → the next APPROVED (mean, median and longest, counted in the window their *end* event falls in, `?days=` 7–730, default 90); a **مرجوع ends the attempt** — the days a returned document then spends as a draft belong to no step, and its next submit starts a fresh one; **return rate** is RETURNED ÷ (CONFIRMED + APPROVED + RETURNED), "of every decision a reviewer took, how many sent it back"; **waiting now** is the count per step and the oldest wait. Projects: **company progress is weighted** — Σ weight(DONE) ÷ Σ weight(not CANCELLED) across every unfinished (ACTIVE or ON_HOLD, not archived) project the viewer may read, *not* the mean of per-project percentages (a 6-weight project at 100 % and a 2-weight one at 50 % is 88 %, not 75 %); **workload** is open ریزهدف (not DONE/CANCELLED) per assignee, busiest first, top 15, with how many are overdue. The project half goes through `visible_projects`, so a person sees only the projects they could see anyway and the screen says so; because of that, and because the queries are aggregates, nothing is cached. Nothing measured shows as a dash, never a misleading `0`. Frontend: a `KpiPanel` above the exports on `/reports` — a window selector (۳۰/۹۰/۱۸۰/۳۶۵ days), stat cards with their definitions, waiting-now, a weighted progress bar, and workload bars; `lib/reports.ts` holds the types and the formatting (`formatDuration` picks hours or days, `formatPercent`, Persian digits), 6 new vitest (339 total). ### Phase 12 — live paper for دستورالعمل / روش اجرایی / پوستر (plan approved 2026-09-28)
- **Unsaved work survives a closed tab.** While a designer has unsaved changes they are kept in this browser (1 s after typing pauses; per user and document). - **Fix: printed documents no longer lose text** (the owner's decision, 2026-09-28 — the two text-losing quirks of the original algorithm, kept on purpose until now). Body lines **wrap by their real width**: V_1.0 left a 90–170 character line whole (it ran off the left edge of the page) and cut longer ones into 70-character chunks mid-word; now a line that fits is drawn exactly as before, and one that does not is broken between words before it is shaped, with bold/italic/underline markers closed and reopened across the break and every piece of a heading kept bold. **`text_merge` keeps every word**: the responsibilities descriptions and the control table's free text dropped the word that forced a wrap and printed a chunk twice when a short word repeated. Name search normalises Arabic/Persian letters, digits and Unicode whitespace *in SQL* (no stored copy, no data migration); a test pins the SQL against `normalize_search_term`.
- **Organisation tree (Phase 7, slice 7.1 — backend only)**: new `apps.organization` with `Company` (singleton, `pk=1`) and `OrgNode` — one self-referencing table for شرکت / حوزه / واحد / بخش with a materialised `path` and stored `depth`. A واحد may hang directly under the company (a company with no حوزه is valid). The parent-kind table, the single root and sibling-unique names (on the normalised key, so Arabic and Persian yeh collide) live in the database as constraints; `tree.py` owns everything a CHECK cannot see (path/depth, cycle check on move, delete pre-flight) and checks first so callers get Persian errors. API: `GET /org/tree/` (flat, depth-first, one query; capped by `ORG_TREE_MAX_NODES`, over it two levels + `truncated` and `?parent=` lazy loading), `/org/nodes/` CRUD + `archive/` + `unarchive/`, `/org/company/` and its logo. Reads: any signed-in user. Writes: `manage_organization`. Nothing can create the `Company` row yet — bootstrap arrives in slice 7.4.
