### Phase 12 — live paper for دستورالعمل / روش اجرایی / پوستر (plan approved 2026-09-28)
- **Unsaved work survives a closed tab.** While a designer has unsaved changes they are kept in this browser (1 s after typing pauses; per user and document). Opening the document again offers «تغییرات ذخیره‌نشده‌ای از … پیدا شد — بازیابی / نادیده گرفتن»; the restore is one undo step. Work left on a version someone has since saved is **not** offered (restoring would overwrite theirs) — a note says it was set aside. The stored copy is removed once nothing is unsaved (after a save, or undoing back). Storage failures (private browsing, full quota) never show as errors. Both designers; `lib/designer-draft.ts`.
- **Undo and redo in both designers.** ↶ «واگرد» / ↷ «ازنو» beside the save buttons, and **Ctrl/⌘+Z**, **Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Z** or **Ctrl+Y** for the whole document, text fields included (matched on the physical key, so it works with a Persian keyboard layout). Changes less than 0.6 s apart are one step, so a burst of typing is undone at once; up to 100 steps; saving keeps the history. Uploads (logo, files) are saved outright and are not undone. The history is `lib/undo-history.ts`.
- **Drag blocks to reorder them.** Each block of دستورالعمل / روش اجرایی / پوستر has a grip (⠿) beside its title: drag it above or below another block (a line shows where it lands); ↑/↓ stay for keyboard and touch. The drag logic is now one hook (`components/designer/useDragReorder.ts`) shared with the form canvas, and the list helpers moved to `lib/reorder.ts` (re-exported from `lib/form-designer.ts`).
- **«ذخیره» keeps you on the page** (the owner's decision, 2026-09-28, replacing V_1.0's return-to-list): it saves, says «مستند … ذخیره شد» in the save bar and leaves the editor and the live paper open. A new **«ذخیره و بازگشت»** saves and returns to «ساخت مستند» with the message there, as every save used to. Both the block designer and the form designer.
- **The live paper beside the block editor.** دستورالعمل, روش اجرایی and پوستر (and older فرم documents that still have blocks) are now edited next to their pages *exactly as they print*: while there are unsaved edits the paper is redrawn from them by the server's own renderer about 0.8 s after typing stops (a stale request is cancelled, the previous pages stay on screen dimmed while the new ones draw, unchanged pages are not re-sent); otherwise it shows what is stored — so a reviewer or a finished document sees its paper too. Picking or removing the logo redraws it. «هم‌عرض ستون» / «اندازهٔ چاپ» zoom, «صفحه n از N» under each page, and a note past 20 pages. From `xl` the paper is a sticky column beside the editor; below it the page has «ویرایش | پیش‌نمایش» tabs (both stay mounted) and fits 375 px. `apiPost` accepts an `AbortSignal`.
- **Live preview endpoint.** `POST /documents/{id}/live-preview/` returns a document's pages as images, drawn by the same renderers that print it — from the **unsaved** edits when the body is sent (the content PUT's JSON; needs `create_document` and a draft, and answers `version_conflict` / `content_locked` like a save), otherwise from what is stored. The edits are saved inside a transaction that is always rolled back, so the whole real pipeline runs and nothing is kept — no rows, no `PdfBuild`, no file, no `on_commit` file deletion. Pages are rasterised with **PDFium (`pypdfium2==5.13.0`, new dependency)** at ~115 dpi as quantised PNGs (~15–30 KB a page), at most 20 pages; the client sends the hashes it already shows and gets images only for changed pages. Rate limit `LIVE_PREVIEW_RATELIMIT_RATE` (60/m per user) answers 429 in Persian. `.env.example` no longer advertises running several stacks side by side (one stack, `v_20`).
- **Fix: printed documents no longer lose text** (the owner's decision, 2026-09-28 — the two text-losing quirks of the original algorithm, kept on purpose until now). Body lines **wrap by their real width**: V_1.0 left a 90–170 character line whole (it ran off the left edge of the page) and cut longer ones into 70-character chunks mid-word; now a line that fits is drawn exactly as before, and one that does not is broken between words before it is shaped, with bold/italic/underline markers closed and reopened across the break and every piece of a heading kept bold. **`text_merge` keeps every word**: the responsibilities descriptions and the control table's free text dropped the word that forced a wrap and printed a chunk twice when a short word repeated. The golden-file oracle applies exactly these two fixes to V_1.0's own code (a documented third normalisation in `make_golden_from_v1.py`); three of the four golden files are unchanged byte for byte, and `full` (which has long lines) was regenerated.
### Phase 11 — form designer for فرم (FR) documents (ADR-011)
- **Fix: a فرم created on the old code opened the old designer even when empty.** Migration `documents/0005` gives every existing document `body_kind = blocks`, so the owner's new «فرم استخدام» draft kept the five block types. Migration `documents/0006` turns every **empty, never-revised فرم draft** into a form (default page settings); a فرم with content, a later revision or anything past draft keeps its blocks, as ADR-011 decided.
- **Fix: signatures in a form's approval strip sat at the left of their cells.** Platypus left-aligns a flowable narrower than its cell; the strip now centres its cells. Found by building the official PDF of an approved form (three sign-offs with drawn signatures) through Celery.
- **Document defaults in «تنظیمات».** The empty «تنظیمات» page now holds «پیش‌فرض‌های مستندات» (decided with the owner 2026-09-26: company-wide defaults; starter templates and snippets were not chosen): the logo (the company's own, `/org/company/logo/`), the two footnotes, and for forms the header subtitle and the «شماره / تاریخ / پیوست» box. Every **new** document starts from them — logo (a copy) and footnotes for every group, the subtitle and letter box for a فرم; they are copied at creation, so changing them later never alters an existing or issued document, and a revision still copies its predecessor. Stored on `Company` (migration `organization/0003`: `doc_footnote1`, `doc_footnote2`, `form_subtitle`, `form_show_letter_box`), shown under `document_defaults` in `GET /org/company/`, edited with `PATCH /org/company/document-defaults/` (`manage_organization`; unlike the profile it never moves the setup wizard's bookmark). The documents app still reads nothing of the organisation's: `documents.services.NEW_DOCUMENT_HOOKS` is filled by the organisation app at start-up (`organization/document_defaults.py`), which keeps the Phase 7 structural guard true. `LogoSection` now takes the endpoint, so the designers and «تنظیمات» share it. Tests: backend +11 (1086 on the merged tree).
- **Forms: choice questions, rating grids and Q&A lists** — the elements questionnaires and evaluations need. **گزینه‌ای**: a question and 1–20 options with square (چندگزینه‌ای) or round (تک‌گزینه‌ای) marks, laid out on one flowing line (wrapping right to left), one per line, or in 2–4 columns, with an optional «سایر: ……». **جدول ارزیابی**: items as rows (numbered, up to 40) and 2–7 grades as columns (default عالی / خوب / متوسط / ضعیف) with a mark in every cell, an optional «توضیحات» column, its header repeated across pages. **پرسش‌ها**: up to 100 numbered questions, each with its answer room — ruled lines, a frame, «بله / خیر» boxes, or none — and never separated from it by a page break. Tests: backend +9 (998); the palette test covers the new kinds' defaults (frontend 204).
- **Forms: merged table cells.** A table's `merges` join rectangles of cells in the header and the written rows — e.g. a two-row header with «مدت همکاری» over «از تاریخ / تا تاریخ» and «ردیف» spanning both rows. A merge covers at least two cells, stays inside the header or inside the body (so a repeated header is whole), never overlaps another (the message names both), and blank rows cannot be merged. The PDF mirrors each range for right-to-left (platypus `SPAN`), draws its text once, and grows the spanned rows when the text needs more room. **In the designer:** select a cell of the selected table and use «ادغام با خانهٔ چپ» / «ادغام با خانهٔ پایین» (works on touch), Shift+click for a range and «ادغام خانه‌های انتخاب‌شده», and «جدا کردن»; a merge swallows any merge it touches and keeps the first text. Removing, moving or adding columns and header / written rows moves or drops merges so they stay valid. **Stored elements are read through the current schema:** an element or page settings saved before a property existed now load and print with its default (a table saved before merges read as `merges: []` — the designer crashed on it and the PDF would have failed); stored data that breaks newer rules is shown as stored. Tests: backend +12 (989), frontend +8 (204).
- **Forms: tables with resizable columns.** New element **جدول**: up to 20 columns (widths in percent adding up to 100, each ≥ 4) with a type each — متن, شمارهٔ ردیف (1, 2, 3 … printed down the body), گزینهٔ تیک‌زدنی (a box in every empty cell) or تاریخ («/ /» in every empty cell) — and an alignment; a 1–3 row header (so slice 6 can merge cells into grouped headers); rows written in advance plus up to 300 blank rows to fill by hand; row height, font size, lines (همهٔ خطوط / فقط قاب / فقط افقی / بدون خط), a shaded header, and a header that **repeats on every page** of a long table; an optional title that never ends a page on its own. Column 1 is printed on the right; a row with long text grows, blank rows keep their height. **In the designer:** while a table is selected, its header and written cells are typed into on the canvas, and **the border between two columns is dragged to resize them** (pointer events; the total stays 100; double-click evens them out); the inspector adds, removes and moves columns (a new column takes half of the widest, a removed one gives its width to its neighbour) and sets the header / written / blank row counts. Tests: backend +12 (977), frontend +6 (196).
- **Forms: labelled blanks, answer boxes and signature boxes.** Three element kinds. **فیلدها** — rows of «برچسب: ______» cells (up to 40 rows × 6 cells, widths in percent adding up to 100 per row); a cell's type picks its blank: a line (ممتد / نقطه‌چین / کادر), a Jalali date «…/…/…», 10 digit boxes for کد ملی, 11 for تلفن همراه, or a tick box; an optional **3×4 photo box** («محل الصاق عکس») sits on the left and the grid splits across pages between rows (the photo stays with the first rows). **کادر پاسخ** — an optional title over ruled lines (0–25, dotted or solid) or an empty frame of a chosen height, never taller than a page. **امضا** — 1–4 signature boxes, each with an optional «نام و نام خانوادگی» and «تاریخ» line, right to left, plus an optional «محل مهر» box. The inspector edits rows and cells (add, remove, reorder rows, «پهنای برابر», a cell's width taken from its neighbour so the row stays at 100). Tests: backend +13 (965), frontend +6 (190).
- **A new فرم is authored in a form designer and prints as a compact form** (ADR-011, decided with the owner 2026-09-26: printable blank form, compact layout, block builder on a live A4 canvas). `Document.body_kind` (`blocks` | `form`, migration `documents/0005`) is fixed at creation — a new فرم gets `form`, everything else and every existing or imported فرم keeps `blocks` — and revisions inherit it; `Document.form_settings` holds the page settings (orientation, base font size, approval strip, header subtitle / company name / letter box). A form body is `Section` rows of the new type `Form Element` whose `content` is `{kind, …}`, validated and normalised by `apps/documents/form_schema.py` (Persian messages naming the element: «جزء ۲ (عنوان بخش): …»; caps: 300 elements, 5000-character text); this slice has the structural kinds **عنوان بخش** (band / underline / plain, levels 1–3, automatic numbering 1., 2.1.), **متن** (rich-text markers, alignment, size, optional frame), **خط جداکننده** (solid / dashed / dotted / double, thickness, spacing), **فاصله** and **شکست صفحه**. The content API refuses block types in a form and form elements in a block document; block documents keep their 100-block cap. **PDF:** `pdfgen/render.py` picks the renderer by body kind; the owner's `renderer.py` is untouched (goldens still byte-equal). Tests: backend +48 (952), frontend +12 (184).
